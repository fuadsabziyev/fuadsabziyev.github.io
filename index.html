FOK Uploader

FOK (Financial Review Cast / Финансовый обзор каст / Maliyyə icmalı) is a daily podcast and news digest about business and finance in Azerbaijan, published on YouTube and Telegram (t.me/FOK_Fin_obzor).

FOK Uploader is a private desktop tool used only by the FOK channel owner. It uploads the channel's own daily videos and Shorts to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API, and fills in their title, description, tags, playlist and publish time.

What the app does

  • Uploads video files created by FOK to the FOK YouTube channel.
  • Sets video metadata (title, description, tags, language, category, playlist, scheduled publish time).
  • Reads the channel's own playlists to place videos in them.

The app is not offered to the public, has no user accounts and does not access anyone else's data.
